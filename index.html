Philosophy

How I Think About Product

"

Use AI where it creates magic.
Use rules where it creates reliability.

— My guiding principle for every AI product decision

The Formula
Excellence = User Value + Business Impact + Team Growth
🤖
Agentic-first thinking

Design for orchestration and autonomy from day one. Don't bolt agents onto existing workflows — rethink the workflow for what agents make possible.

📈
Hill-climbing on probabilistic products

AI products are probabilistic — perfection isn't the goal, improvement is. Ship fast, measure relentlessly, and use data to navigate toward the right peak.

🛡️
Responsible AI as a first-class metric

Trust, fairness, and explainability aren't post-launch audits — they're launch criteria. An AI product that erodes trust isn't a product worth shipping.

🤝
Inclusive product thinking

The best products serve the widest range of users, not just the loudest ones. Diversity of perspective in the room directly improves the product on the screen.

🧩
Cross-functional influence over authority

Great products aren't mandated — they're earned through clarity, evidence, and trust. The PM's job is to make the right thing the obvious thing for every team in the room.

⚡
Team growth is a product output

A PM who ships great products but leaves the team worse off has failed. The best product leaders make everyone around them better — engineers, designers, and stakeholders alike.
